How Tagalog verb conjugation works

🎯
5 Focuses
Tagalog marks who is in the spotlight — the doer (Actor), what's being acted on (Object), the place (Locative), the beneficiary (Benefactive), or the tool used (Instrumental). Not every root accepts every focus.
⏳
4 Aspects
Instead of tenses, Tagalog uses aspects: action that's incomplete / ongoing, completed (past), contemplated (future), or the infinitive (to-verb).
🔁
Affixes, not auxiliaries
Rather than helper words like "will" or "was", Tagalog adds prefixes, infixes, and suffixes directly to the root — and often reduplicates the first syllable to mark ongoing / future.
